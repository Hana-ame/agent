## AI / LLM Trends
- **AI as a Philosophical Debate and Self-Learning Tool**
  Users widely employ LLMs (DeepSeek, Doubao, Claude) to discuss theology, comparative religion, and philosophy, replacing traditional deep textbook browsing.
- **Model Behavioral Characteristics**
  - Strong logical debate and deep reasoning capabilities in long contexts.
  - Potential sycophancy or self-censorship tendencies observed on controversial topics.
- **Prompt and Interaction Techniques**
  - Socratic questioning: Users guide the model through iterative questioning for deeper exploration.
  - Self-reflection prompts: Prompting the model to challenge its own previous response for balanced insights.

## User Perspectives
- **Original Poster**: Finds debating with AI intellectually stimulating and efficient, while emphasizing that independent human thought is required to avoid passive echo chambers.
- **Community Members**: Note that AI synthesizes established historical discussions well, democratizing knowledge while demanding active critical thinking from users.
